# Singularity — Investor Overview

## What is Singularity?

Singularity is a **fully autonomous enterprise operating system** — not a chatbot, not an assistant, not a copilot. It is a self-healing, self-optimizing runtime that audits, delegates, monitors, and evolves without human intervention. It manages everything from code quality to infrastructure health to financial tracking across organizations from 1 person to 30,000.

---

## Vision

Every company should have a **live digital twin** — an accurate, real-time model of its people, processes, markets, and operations that can simulate any future scenario before it happens.

## The Problem We Solve

Traditional ERP systems are **static templates** — they record transactions but cannot model reality. They don't understand your people, your capacity, your market dynamics, or your future risks.

Singularity solves this by integrating:
1. **Real-Time Vectorization** — every file, document, and data point ingested continuously into vector state (updated every N seconds)
2. **Digital Twin Engine** — true granular mapping of company departments, roles, people, skills, capacity
3. **Simulation Analysis** — run "what-if" scenarios (product launches, campaigns, organizational changes) with predicted business and customer impact
4. **Autonomous Intelligence** — competitive model (MoE transformer) analyzing market positioning, threats, win/loss patterns

---

## How It Works

Every file in the enterprise directory (`singularity/erp/` and `enterprise/`) is continuously ingested by redundant vector workers. The digital twin uses these real-time embeddings to build accurate simulations. The competitive model transforms these vectors into strategic intelligence.
